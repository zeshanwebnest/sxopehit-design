Investment & Citizenship

Citizenship by Investment for Pakistanis

Secure a powerful second passport with visa-free travel, financial freedom and global mobility.

What is Citizenship by Investment?

If you hold a Pakistani passport you already know the situation — visa rejections, long embassy visits, limited travel options and constant uncertainty. A second passport changes that: more freedom to travel, fewer restrictions and easier access to opportunities.

Donation-based. You make a non-refundable contribution to a government fund. No returns, but full citizenship in exchange.

Real estate investment. You purchase approved property in the target country. In many cases the investment can be sold after a holding period.

Business investment. You invest in a local business or enterprise that creates jobs and drives economic growth.

Know The Difference

Golden Visa vs Citizenship

Many people confuse residency by investment with investment citizenship. They are very different, and choosing the wrong one can cost you years.

FeatureCitizenship by InvestmentResidency (Golden Visa)
Second passportYes, a full passport issuedNo, only a residency permit
TimelineImmediate (3–6 months)Years of residency required first
Visa-free travelHigh — 100 to 150+ countriesLimited, depends on your original passport
Right to live abroadNot requiredUsually requires physical presence
Family inclusionYes, spouse and children includedYes, but with more conditions
Long-term valuePermanent citizenshipMay require renewal
