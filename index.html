Frequently Asked

Answers OfYour Common Questions

Everything applicants ask us most often, answered plainly. Still unsure about something? Our advisors reply within one business day.

We match your skills, qualifications and experience against live vacancies from our partner employers abroad, then support you through applications, interviews and the work-permit process.

It starts with a free profile assessment, followed by CV preparation, employer shortlisting, interview coaching and, once you have an offer, full visa and relocation support.

Most applications need a valid passport, academic transcripts, proof of funds, an offer letter from your institution, language test results and medical clearance. You receive a personalised checklist after your first consultation.

Timelines vary by destination, but most student visas are decided within four to twelve weeks once a complete application is submitted. We advise applying well ahead of your intake date.

Requirements depend on the country and the stream, and are usually based on age, education, work experience, language ability and proof of funds. Your free assessment will confirm which routes you qualify for.
