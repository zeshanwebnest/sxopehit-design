UK Immigration

Why Pakistanis Choose to Immigrate to the UK

The UK's points-based system gives a transparent, pass/fail route into the country — through the Skilled Worker, Global Talent, Innovator Founder and family visa categories — with a defined five-year path to settlement.

A route for many profiles. Sponsored skilled work, unsponsored talent routes for recognised leaders, business founder visas and family reunification all sit under one transparent framework, rather than a single narrow category.

A clear five-year route to settlement. Most work and family visa holders can apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain after five years of continuous residence, with British citizenship available a year later.

  • Skilled Worker & Health and Care Worker routes
  • Global Talent & Innovator Founder visas
  • Family & spouse visa pathways
  • NHS healthcare & free state education for dependants
  • Five-year route to settlement (ILR)
  • Large, established Pakistani diaspora

How We Work

Our UK Immigration Process

Our step-by-step procedure once you appoint us as your immigration adviser.

01

Route & Points Assessment

We identify the strongest visa route for your profile — Skilled Worker, Global Talent, Innovator Founder or family — and score it against the 70-point threshold.

02

Sponsorship & Endorsement

We help you secure a Certificate of Sponsorship from a licensed employer, or prepare your endorsement application for talent or founder routes.

03

Documentation & Funds

We prepare your English test evidence, qualifications, criminal record certificate and proof of funds consistent with Home Office requirements.

04

Lodgement & Tracking

We submit your visa application and track it with you through to a decision, then guide you on the route to settlement afterward.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical UK immigration application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your visa route at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport
  • Certificate of Sponsorship (job offer reference)
  • English test certificate (IELTS or equivalent)
  • Proof of qualifications
  • Criminal record certificate (where required)
  • Proof of funds/maintenance
  • TB test certificate (where applicable)
  • CV & employment history
