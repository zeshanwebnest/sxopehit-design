Canadian Immigration

Why Pakistanis Choose to Immigrate to Canada

Canada's Express Entry system grants permanent residence from day one — not a temporary work visa first — through a transparent points-based ranking that rewards age, education, language and experience.

Permanent residency, not a stepping-stone visa. Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker, Canadian Experience Class) and Provincial Nominee Programs land you as a permanent resident directly, with your family included from the start.

Multiple parallel routes. Beyond Express Entry, Provincial Nominee Programs, family sponsorship and caregiver pathways mean more ways to qualify than a single visa category — we help you find the route that fits your profile.

  • Express Entry: Federal Skilled Worker & Canadian Experience Class
  • Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP)
  • Family sponsorship pathways
  • Universal public healthcare for PRs and families
  • Clear route to Canadian citizenship
  • Large, established Pakistani community

How We Work

Our Canadian Immigration Process

Our step-by-step procedure once you appoint us as your immigration adviser.

01

CRS Score & Route Assessment

We calculate your Comprehensive Ranking System score and identify whether Express Entry, PNP or family sponsorship gives you the strongest route.

02

ECA & Language Testing

We guide you through your Educational Credential Assessment and language test (IELTS/CELPIP) — the two levers that move your score the most.

03

Profile & Nomination

We create your Express Entry profile and pursue provincial nomination where it strengthens your application, adding up to 600 points.

04

Invitation & Application

Once invited, we help you submit a complete application with medicals, police certificates and proof of funds, and track it through to a decision.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Canadian immigration application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your route at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport
  • Language test results (IELTS/CELPIP/TEF)
  • Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)
  • Police clearance certificates
  • Medical exam from an approved physician
  • Proof of settlement funds
  • Job offer/LMIA (where applicable)
  • CV & employment reference letters
