Australian Immigration

Why Pakistanis Choose to Immigrate to Australia

Australia's points-based skilled migration system gives transparent, assessable routes to permanent residency — backed by a strong economy, Medicare healthcare and one of the world's highest standards of living.

Multiple, well-defined pathways. From Skilled Independent (189) and State Nominated (190) visas to the Business Innovation and Investment (188) visa and partner/family routes, Australia offers a route for a wide range of profiles.

A points system you can plan around. Unlike a lottery, eligibility is assessed against a clear points scale — age, English ability, work experience and education all count, so we can tell you honestly where you stand before you apply.

  • Points-based skilled migration (189 / 190 / 491)
  • Business & Investor visa (Subclass 188) pathway
  • Partner & family visa options
  • World-class healthcare & quality of life
  • Clear route to permanent residency
  • Large, established Pakistani community

How We Work

Our Australian Immigration Process

Our step-by-step procedure once you appoint us as your immigration adviser.

01

Points & Eligibility Assessment

We score your profile against the 65-point threshold and identify the strongest visa subclass — skilled, business or family — with honest, realistic feedback.

02

Expression of Interest

We prepare and submit your Expression of Interest through SkillSelect, positioning your profile for a state nomination or invitation.

03

Nomination & Documentation

Once nominated or invited, we help you gather skills assessments, English test results, police clearances and financial evidence.

04

Lodgement & Tracking

We lodge your formal visa application and track it with you through to a decision, keeping you informed at every stage.

Documents You'll Need

A typical Australian immigration application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your visa subclass at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport & photographs
  • Skills assessment from a relevant authority
  • English test score (IELTS/PTE)
  • Evidence of work experience
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Health examination results
  • Proof of funds/assets (business & investor visas)
  • Expression of Interest (SkillSelect) submission
