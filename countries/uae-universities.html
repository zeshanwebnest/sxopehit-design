Study in the UAE

Why Pakistani Students Choose the UAE

The UAE combines branch campuses of respected Western universities with a short flight from Pakistan, tax-free income potential and real pathways to long-term residency for top graduates.

Globally recognised degrees, close to home. Institutions such as Heriot-Watt Dubai, Middlesex Dubai and the American University of Sharjah offer internationally accredited degrees without the long flight to the UK or US.

A growing residency ladder for graduates. From the Job Seeker Visa to the Green and Golden Visas, high-achieving graduates have multiple routes to stay and build a career in the UAE.

  • Close proximity & short flight time from Pakistan
  • Tax-free income & global business exposure
  • Branch campuses of recognised Western universities
  • Safe, modern, multicultural environment
  • Strong internship links with multinational companies
  • Pathway to UAE Golden/Green Visa for top graduates

How We Work

Student Visa Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your UAE university-sponsored student visa.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and shortlist accredited universities across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah that match your course and budget.

02

Offer & Attestation

Once admitted, we help you arrange embassy/MOFA attestation of your academic certificates — a required step before your university's PRO processes your visa.

03

Fees & Documentation

We help you confirm tuition payment and prepare the documents your university's PRO submits on your behalf.

04

Arrival, Medical & Emirates ID

After arrival, we guide you through the medical fitness test and Emirates ID application that complete your residency formalities.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical UAE student visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport (6+ months) & copies
  • Passport-size photographs
  • University offer/admission letter
  • Proof of tuition payment
  • Attested academic certificates & transcripts
  • Medical fitness test (after arrival)
  • Emirates ID application
  • Visa form processed by the university's PRO
