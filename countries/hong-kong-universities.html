Study in Hong Kong

Why Pakistani Students Choose Hong Kong

Hong Kong combines world top-100 universities with a two-year, no-job-offer-required post-study work visa — a rare combination that makes it one of Asia's strongest study destinations.

Globally ranked, English-taught universities. HKU, HKUST and CUHK consistently rank among the world's top institutions, with degrees recognised across Asia and internationally.

A straightforward stay-back route. The IANG scheme gives graduates 24 months to live and work in Hong Kong with no job offer or sector restriction needed — among the most flexible post-study visas in Asia.

  • World top-100 ranked universities (HKU, HKUST, CUHK)
  • Gateway location bridging China & global business
  • 24-month IANG post-study work visa
  • No job offer required for IANG eligibility
  • Shorter flight time from Pakistan than Europe/NA
  • Currently uncapped part-time work rights (through 2026)

How We Work

Student Visa/Entry Permit Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your Hong Kong student visa application.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and shortlist Hong Kong's top-ranked universities that match your programme and budget.

02

Offer & Form ID995A

Once admitted, we help you and your institution complete Form ID995A — the core application for your Student Visa/Entry Permit.

03

Funds & Documentation

We help you evidence financial means (typically HK$100,000–150,000/year) and prepare academic transcripts and English test results.

04

Lodge & Track

We submit your application to the Immigration Department and track it with you — applying early is essential as peak season (May–August) can extend processing.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Hong Kong student visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Completed Form ID995A
  • Admission/offer letter from the institution
  • Valid passport & photograph
  • Proof of financial means
  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • English proficiency test result (if required)
  • Proof of accommodation (if under 18)
  • Visa fee receipt
