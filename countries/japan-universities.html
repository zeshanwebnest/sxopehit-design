Study in Japan

Why Pakistani Students Choose Japan

Japan offers affordable, standardised tuition at its national universities, world-class engineering and technology programmes, and a fully-funded MEXT scholarship route for top applicants.

Low, standardised tuition. National universities charge the same fee for local and international students — around ¥535,800 a year — making Japan one of the most affordable developed-country destinations.

A real route into Japan's job market. The Designated Activities (job-hunting) visa gives graduates up to 12 months to find work, in a country with strong demand for skilled foreign talent.

  • Affordable tuition at national universities
  • MEXT scholarships cover full tuition + stipend
  • World-class engineering & technology programmes
  • High safety & quality of life
  • Up to 12-month post-study job-hunting visa
  • Strong demand for foreign skilled workers

How We Work

Student Visa & COE Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your Japan student visa application.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and shortlist national or private universities, including MEXT scholarship-eligible programmes where applicable.

02

Certificate of Eligibility

Once admitted, your institution applies to Japan's Immigration Services Agency for your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) — the key document your visa is built around.

03

Funds & Guarantor Documentation

We help you prepare proof of financial means and, where needed, a guarantor's letter of sponsorship and proof of relationship.

04

Visa Stamp & Pre-Departure

Once your COE is issued, we help you lodge the visa application at the embassy — typically approved within about a week — and prepare you for arrival.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Japan student visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Certificate of Eligibility (COE)
  • Valid passport & visa application form
  • Acceptance letter from the Japanese institution
  • Proof of financial means
  • Letter of guarantee/sponsorship (if applicable)
  • Academic transcripts & diplomas
  • Passport photograph
  • Proof of relationship with guarantor (if applicable)
