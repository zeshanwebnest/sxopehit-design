Study in New Zealand

Why Pakistani Students Choose New Zealand

New Zealand pairs globally recognised, QS-ranked universities with a safe, welcoming environment and a clear post-study work visa of up to three years.

A genuine stay-back pathway. Graduates of an eligible Level 7+ qualification can apply for a Post-Study Work Visa valid for up to three years — real time to find skilled employment.

Exceptional value for PhD study. New Zealand charges PhD students close to domestic (citizen) tuition rates — a standout saving compared to most other English-speaking destinations.

  • Globally recognised, QS-ranked universities
  • Post-study work visa up to 3 years
  • Safe, politically stable, English-speaking
  • Welcoming, growing Pakistani community
  • PhD tuition priced near domestic rates
  • Scenic, low-crime lifestyle

How We Work

Student Visa Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your New Zealand student visa application.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and shortlist NZQA-approved institutions, including PhD options priced near domestic rates where relevant.

02

Offer of Place & Fees

Once admitted, we help you arrange payment or coverage of tuition fees, which your Offer of Place confirms to Immigration New Zealand.

03

Funds & Documentation

We help you evidence living costs of at least NZD 20,000 a year and arrange any required medical exam or police clearance.

04

Lodge & Track

We submit Form INZ 1012 and track your application with you — applying around three months ahead is best, especially during the October–March peak season.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical New Zealand student visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Completed visa application form (INZ 1012)
  • Valid passport & photographs
  • Offer of Place from an NZQA-approved institution
  • Evidence of tuition fees paid or covered
  • Proof of funds for living costs (NZD 20,000/year)
  • Medical examination certificate (if required)
  • Police clearance certificate (stays of 24+ months)
  • Travel/health insurance & return travel proof
