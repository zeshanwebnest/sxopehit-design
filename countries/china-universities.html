Study in China

Why Pakistani Students Choose China

China pairs rapidly rising global rankings with low tuition and generous CSC government scholarships — backed by one of the largest, most established Pakistani student communities abroad.

World-class, fast-rising universities. Institutions such as Tsinghua, Peking and Zhejiang now rank among the world's best, with strong programmes in engineering, medicine and business.

Scholarships that cover the full cost. The Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) can cover tuition, accommodation and a monthly stipend for qualifying students — a major draw for Pakistani applicants.

  • Low tuition with generous CSC scholarships
  • Rapidly rising global university rankings
  • Strong engineering, medicine & business programmes
  • Large, established Pakistani student community
  • Lower cost of living than US/Western Europe
  • Valuable for China–Pakistan trade & CPEC-linked careers

How We Work

X1 Student Visa Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your Chinese X1 student visa application.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and shortlist universities, including CSC scholarship-eligible programmes, that match your field and budget.

02

Admission Notice & JW Form

Once admitted, your institution issues an Admission Notice plus a JW201 (government-funded) or JW202 (self-funded) form — the core documents for your X1 visa.

03

Funds & Documentation

We help you evidence financial means or your scholarship award letter, and prepare academic transcripts and health documentation.

04

Lodge & Track

We submit your X1 visa application and track it with you until a decision, then guide you through the residence permit conversion within 30 days of arrival.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical X1 visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Admission Notice from the Chinese institution
  • JW201 or JW202 form
  • Valid passport & photographs
  • Proof of funds or CSC scholarship award letter
  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • Physical examination/health certificate
  • Completed visa application form
  • Visa fee receipt
