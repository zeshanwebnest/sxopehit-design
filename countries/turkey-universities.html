Study in Turkey

Why Pakistani Students Choose Turkey

Turkey offers low-cost public university tuition and a culturally familiar, Muslim-majority environment, backed by strong Pakistan–Turkey diplomatic and scholarship ties.

Quality education at a low cost. Public universities such as Istanbul University, METU and Ankara University charge some of the lowest tuition in Europe and Asia for the education quality on offer.

A natural cultural fit. Halal food is widely available, the Pakistani and wider South Asian student community is large and established, and many programmes are taught in English.

  • Muslim-majority country, culturally familiar
  • Low-cost public university tuition
  • Many English-taught bachelor's/master's programmes
  • Strong Pakistan–Turkey diplomatic & scholarship ties
  • Gateway location between Europe & Asia
  • Large, established Pakistani student community

How We Work

Student Visa & Residence Permit Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your Turkish student visa and residence permit.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and shortlist public and foundation/private universities that match your field, budget and language preference.

02

Admission Letter & Visa Application

Once admitted, we help you prepare your original acceptance letter and lodge your Öğrenci Vizesi application at the Turkish consulate.

03

Funds, Insurance & Accommodation

We help you evidence financial means, arrange travel/health insurance and secure proof of accommodation — a dorm letter or rental agreement.

04

Arrival & Residence Permit

After arrival, we guide you through converting your entry visa into a Student Residence Permit via e-ikamet and the Provincial Directorate of Migration Management.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Turkish student visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport (18+ months validity recommended)
  • Original university acceptance letter
  • Completed visa application form
  • Proof of financial means
  • Proof of accommodation in Turkey
  • Travel/health insurance
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Visa fee payment receipt
