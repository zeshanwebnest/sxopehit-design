Study in Australia

Why Pakistani Students Choose Australia

Australia pairs globally ranked universities with a clear pathway from study to post-study work and, for many graduates, permanent residency — all within a safe, multicultural environment.

World-class, globally ranked universities. Group of Eight institutions such as the University of Melbourne, University of Sydney, ANU, UNSW and Monash sit consistently among the world's best, and Australian qualifications are recognised everywhere.

A structured route after graduation. The Subclass 500 Student visa leads into the Temporary Graduate (Subclass 485) visa, giving eligible graduates two to three years of post-study work rights before considering permanent pathways.

  • Group of Eight & globally ranked universities
  • Clear pathway toward permanent residency
  • Post-study work visa up to 2–3 years
  • Large, established Pakistani community
  • Safe, multicultural cities
  • Part-time work rights offset living costs

How We Work

Subclass 500 Student Visa Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your Australian student visa application.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and budget, then shortlist Group of Eight and other accredited universities that match your goals and course requirements.

02

Offer & CoE

Once you accept an offer and pay the required deposit, your institution issues a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) — the core document your Subclass 500 application is built around.

03

Funds, OSHC & Genuine Student Statement

We help you evidence sufficient funds, arrange Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) and prepare a coherent Genuine Student statement — the single biggest factor in refusals.

04

Lodge & Track

We lodge your Subclass 500 application, arrange your health examination and track progress with you until a decision — typically within six to nine weeks.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Subclass 500 application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
  • Valid passport & photographs
  • IELTS/PTE/TOEFL score
  • Proof of funds for tuition & living costs
  • Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)
  • Genuine Student (GS) statement
  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • Visa fee receipt & health exam results
