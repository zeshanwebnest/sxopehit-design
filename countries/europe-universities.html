Study in Europe

Why Pakistani Students Choose Europe

From tuition-free public universities in Germany to generous scholarships in Hungary, Europe offers globally recognised degrees at a fraction of the cost of the UK, USA or Australia.

Tuition-free or near-free public education. Germany's public universities charge little more than an administrative fee each semester, and Stipendium Hungaricum, Erasmus+ and DAAD scholarships open the door further.

A genuine post-study stay-back option. Germany offers an 18-month job-seeker residence permit after graduation, and Ireland's Stamp 1G gives up to two years of unrestricted work — no employer sponsor required.

  • Tuition-free or low-cost public universities (Germany)
  • Scholarships: Stipendium Hungaricum, Erasmus+, DAAD
  • 18–24 month post-study job-seeker visas
  • Globally recognised degrees, strong EU job mobility
  • Lower living costs in Hungary & Poland
  • English-taught programmes — no local language needed

How We Work

National (Type D) Visa Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your European national student visa application.

01

Country & University Shortlist

We match your budget and academic profile to the right country — Germany, Hungary, Ireland, Poland or Cyprus — and shortlist accredited universities and scholarship options.

02

Admission & Blocked Account

Once admitted, we help you arrange proof of funds — typically a blocked account or sponsor letter covering roughly one year of living costs.

03

Insurance & Motivation Letter

We arrange your health/travel insurance and craft a clear statement of purpose explaining your study plan — a key document consular officers review closely.

04

Appointment & Lodge

We book your visa appointment early to beat seasonal backlogs, submit biometrics and track your application until a decision.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical European national visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your chosen country at your free consultation.

  • University admission/acceptance letter
  • Valid passport (12+ months validity)
  • Proof of funds (blocked account or sponsor letter)
  • Health/travel insurance
  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • English or local-language proficiency certificate
  • Statement of purpose/motivation letter
  • Visa application form & biometric photos
