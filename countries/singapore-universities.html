Study in Singapore

Why Pakistani Students Choose Singapore

Singapore puts world-ranked universities like NUS and NTU at the centre of a safe, highly organised global hub — with strong finance, tech and business industry links right on campus.

Globally top-ranked, English-medium universities. NUS and NTU consistently rank among the world's best, giving graduates an edge with employers across Asia and beyond.

A strategic launchpad for a regional career. Singapore's position as an ASEAN gateway, combined with a strong Pakistani diaspora, makes it a practical base for students aiming at regional careers in finance or technology.

  • World-ranked universities (NUS, NTU) in a global hub
  • Safe, clean, highly organised city-state
  • Strategic gateway to ASEAN job markets
  • Strong finance, tech & business industry links
  • English-medium instruction throughout
  • Short flight time & strong Pakistani diaspora

How We Work

Student's Pass Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your Singapore Student's Pass application.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile and test scores, then shortlist universities and polytechnics that match your goals — from NUS/NTU to strong private institutions.

02

Offer & SOLAR+ Submission

Once admitted, your institution submits your Student's Pass application via the SOLAR+ system on your behalf.

03

Funds & Documentation

We help you evidence financial support and prepare academic transcripts and any required test scores.

04

Approval & Arrival

Once approved, we guide you through your arrival, the in-person formalities and settling into full-time study in Singapore.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Student's Pass application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Passport photographs
  • Letter of offer/acceptance from the institution
  • Forms 16 & V36 (via SOLAR+)
  • Academic transcripts & certificates
  • Proof of financial support
  • Medical examination report (if required)
  • Standardised test scores (if applicable)
