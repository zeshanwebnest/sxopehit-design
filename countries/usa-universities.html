Study in the USA

Why Pakistani Students Choose the USA

The United States is home to the world's top-ranked universities, the widest range of degree specialisations, and strong scholarship and assistantship opportunities for talented international students.

Unmatched choice and research funding. From Ivy League institutions to well-funded state universities, the US offers more degree programs and research opportunities than any other destination.

Work experience built into your degree. Optional Practical Training (OPT) gives graduates up to 12 months of work authorisation in their field, extended to 36 months for STEM graduates.

  • World's top-ranked universities & research funding
  • Widest range of degree specialisations
  • OPT work authorisation up to 12 months (36 for STEM)
  • Strong alumni networks & global recognition
  • On-campus work & assistantship opportunities
  • Established Pakistani-American community

How We Work

F-1 Student Visa Process

Our step-by-step procedure for your US F-1 visa application.

01

Profile & University Shortlist

We assess your academic profile, test scores and budget, then shortlist SEVP-certified schools that fit your goals — including scholarship and assistantship options.

02

Admission & Form I-20

Once admitted and your finances are verified, your school issues Form I-20, pays the SEVIS I-901 fee and prepares you for the DS-160 online application.

03

DS-160 & Interview Prep

We help you complete the DS-160, pay the MRV fee, book your visa interview and prepare thoroughly for the officer's questions on study intent and ties to Pakistan.

04

Interview & Decision

We coach you right up to interview day. Book as early as possible — interview slots can be scheduled up to 365 days before your program start date.

Documents You'll Need

A typical F-1 visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Form I-20 from your SEVP-certified school
  • SEVIS I-901 fee receipt
  • DS-160 confirmation page
  • Valid passport & visa photo
  • Proof of financial support (1 year tuition & living costs)
  • Academic transcripts & test scores
  • MRV visa fee receipt
  • Evidence of ties to Pakistan
