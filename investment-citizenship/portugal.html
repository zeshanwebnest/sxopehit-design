Portugal Golden Visa (ARI)

Why Pakistani Investors Choose Portugal

Portugal's Golden Visa remains one of the lightest-touch routes into the EU — an average of just seven days a year in the country — with a clear five-year path to EU citizenship.

Fund-based, not real estate. Since October 2023, the real estate route has been closed. Today's qualifying routes are regulated investment funds, cultural donations, R&D contributions and job creation.

A note on timelines. Portugal's immigration agency has faced significant backlogs, so real-world processing often runs well beyond the official target — we set realistic expectations from day one and keep you updated throughout.

  • No real estate required — fund, R&D, donation or job routes
  • Lightest stay requirement of major EU golden visas
  • Visa-free Schengen Area movement
  • Clear 5-year path to EU citizenship
  • Spouse, children & dependent parents included
  • Access to Portugal's EU-standard healthcare & education

Investment Routes

Current Golden Visa Routes

Real estate, renovation-property and the old capital-transfer routes were closed in October 2023 — these are today's active options.

01

Investment Fund

€500,000 in a regulated Portuguese venture capital or private equity fund — the most commonly used route today.

02

Cultural Donation

€250,000 (or €200,000 in low-density regions) — a non-refundable donation to an approved arts or heritage project.

03

Scientific Research

€500,000 (or €400,000 in low-density areas) contributed to an accredited research institution.

04

Job Creation

Creating at least 10 full-time jobs (8 in low-density areas) for Portugal-based workers, with no fixed capital threshold.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Portugal Golden Visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your route at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport
  • Proof of investment (fund/donation/contribution evidence)
  • Apostilled criminal record certificate
  • Proof of funds/source of wealth
  • Portuguese tax identification number (NIF)
  • Portuguese bank account statements
  • Health/travel insurance valid in Portugal
  • Civil documents for included dependants
