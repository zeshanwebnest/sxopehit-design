Cyprus Golden Visa

Why Pakistani Investors Choose Cyprus

Cyprus offers a fast-track, low-presence EU residency permit from a €300,000 investment — strategically placed between Europe, Africa and the Middle East, with a favourable tax regime and no inheritance tax.

One of the lowest-presence EU permits. There is no strict minimum-stay rule — investors need visit Cyprus just one day every two years to keep their residency active.

A genuine route toward EU citizenship. After seven years of residence, investors become eligible to apply for Cypriot — and therefore EU — citizenship.

  • No strict minimum stay requirement
  • Strategic hub between Africa, Europe & the Middle East
  • Favourable tax regime, no wealth or inheritance tax
  • Low crime rate & high quality of life
  • Citizenship eligibility after 7 years' residence
  • Access to EU-standard education & healthcare

Investment Routes

Four Ways to Qualify

Each route requires a minimum of €300,000 (plus VAT, where applicable).

01

Residential Property

Purchase a home valued at €300,000 or more — the most popular route among our Cyprus clients.

02

Commercial Real Estate

Invest in hospitality, retail or corporate property worth €300,000 or more, excluding VAT.

03

Collective Investment Units

€300,000 in units of a Cypriot collective investment organisation.

04

Company Share Capital

€300,000 in share capital of a Cyprus-registered company. Bank deposit (€280,000/5 years) and government bond (€250,000) routes are also available.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Cyprus residency-by-investment application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your route at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport
  • Proof of investment (title deed/certificate)
  • Proof of funds & source of wealth
  • Clean criminal record certificate
  • Health insurance valid in Cyprus
  • Bank reference letter
  • Birth/marriage certificates for dependants
  • Completed application forms & photographs
