Malta Permanent Residence Programme

Why Pakistani Investors Choose Malta

The Malta Permanent Residence Programme (MPRP) grants EU permanent residency and Schengen travel with no minimum-stay requirement — a stable, English-speaking base in the heart of the Mediterranean.

Please note: Malta's investment-based citizenship route was suspended in 2025 following an EU court ruling. The MPRP available today grants permanent residency, not citizenship — we present it accurately as a residency programme, with full details below.

No relocation required. The MPRP has no fixed minimum-day stay requirement, provided you maintain the qualifying property and a genuine connection to Malta.

  • Full Schengen Area travel access
  • No minimum physical-stay requirement
  • Family inclusion: spouse, children & parents
  • Stable EU/eurozone jurisdiction, English widely spoken
  • Access to healthcare & international schooling
  • High safety & quality-of-life reputation

Programme Components

What the MPRP Requires

The Malta Permanent Residence Programme combines several elements rather than a single lump sum.

01

Government Fees

A €60,000 administrative fee for the main applicant, split between submission and approval-in-principle.

02

Qualifying Property

Purchase from €375,000, or lease from €14,000 a year, in Malta or Gozo.

03

Government Contribution

€30,000 if buying your qualifying property, or €60,000 if renting.

04

NGO Donation

A €2,000 donation to an approved Maltese philanthropic, cultural, sport or environmental organisation.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical MPRP application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your profile at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport
  • Proof of funds/source of wealth
  • Apostilled criminal record certificate
  • Medical certificate/proof of health insurance
  • Property purchase or lease contract
  • NGO donation receipt
  • Government fee payment receipts
  • Completed application & biometric photographs
