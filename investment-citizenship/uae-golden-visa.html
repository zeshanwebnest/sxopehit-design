UAE Golden Visa

Why Pakistani Investors Choose the UAE

The UAE Golden Visa grants long-term, renewable residency — up to 10 years — with 100% self-sponsorship, zero personal income tax, and one of the fastest approval timelines of any investment programme.

Please note: the UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residency scheme and does not lead to UAE citizenship under any current rule — we present it accurately as a residency programme, not a citizenship route.

Freedom from the 180-day rule. Unlike a standard UAE residence visa, the Golden Visa is exempt from the usual rule that cancels residency after 180 consecutive days outside the country.

  • Long-term residency (5 or 10 years, renewable)
  • 100% self-sponsorship, no local employer needed
  • Exempt from the 180-day absence cancellation rule
  • Zero personal income tax
  • Sponsor your spouse, children & domestic staff
  • World-class healthcare, education & infrastructure

Investment Routes

Choose Your Route

Real estate remains the most popular route among our UAE clients.

01

Real Estate

AED 2,000,000 minimum in a ready or off-plan property, assessed on total value even if mortgaged.

02

Public/Investment Fund

AED 2,000,000 in a UAE-licensed investment fund, or confirmed minimum AED 250,000 in annual tax paid to the Federal Tax Authority.

03

Entrepreneur / New Project

AED 500,000 in an innovative project approved by an accredited incubator, granting a 5-year visa.

04

Existing Company Stake

A company stake worth AED 2,000,000+ plus a tax-compliance letter, granting the full 10-year visa.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical UAE Golden Visa application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your route at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Proof of investment (title deed/fund certificate)
  • Proof of funds/source of wealth
  • Good conduct/criminal record certificate
  • Passport-size photographs (UAE specification)
  • Medical fitness test (post-approval)
  • Emirates ID application documents
  • Property valuation/DLD title deed copy (real estate route)
