Latvia Residency

Why Pakistani Investors Choose Latvia

From as little as €50,000, Latvia offers one of the lowest-cost entry points to EU residency and full Schengen Area travel — with no mandatory relocation.

The EU's most accessible investment residency. A €50,000 investment in a Latvian company's share capital, plus a one-time state fee, is enough to qualify — a fraction of what most EU programmes require.

No pressure to relocate. There's no fixed annual day-count to simply hold the Temporary Residence Permit; you only need to collect your residence card in person after approval.

  • From €50,000 — one of the lowest EU entry points
  • Full Schengen Area travel access
  • No mandatory minimum stay
  • Family inclusion (spouse & minor children)
  • Fast processing — around 30 days standard
  • Access to EU-standard healthcare & education

Investment Routes

Choose Your Route

Up to 10 investors can co-invest in one qualifying company under the share capital route.

01

Company Share Capital

€50,000 in a Latvian SME (or €100,000 for a larger company) plus a one-time €10,000 state fee — the most affordable route.

02

Real Estate Purchase

€250,000 minimum property value, plus a 5% one-time state acquisition tax.

03

Bank Time Deposit

€280,000 placed in a Latvian bank for a fixed 5-year term.

04

Government Bonds

€250,000 minimum purchase, plus a €38,000 state fee.

Checklist

Documents You'll Need

A typical Latvia residence permit application requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your route at your free consultation.

  • Valid passport
  • Completed TRP application form
  • Proof of investment (contract/bank statement)
  • Proof of funds/source of wealth
  • Apostilled criminal record certificate
  • Valid health insurance (minimum 1 year)
  • Civil documents for dependants (translated)
  • Medical/TB certificate (where applicable)

Latvia's own law only automatically allows dual citizenship with EU/EFTA/NATO countries plus Australia, Brazil and New Zealand. Pakistan isn't on that list, though this only matters if you pursue full naturalisation, not for the residence permit itself.

€50,000 into a Latvian company's share capital (plus a €10,000 state fee) is the lowest entry point; real estate (€250,000+) and bank/bond routes (€250,000–€280,000) are the other main options.

The residence permit itself is typically processed within about one to two months. Latvia has no citizenship-by-investment shortcut — full naturalisation requires ten years of genuine residence with language and civics exams.

No day-count is required just to hold the temporary residence permit, but 183+ days a year is required later if you want to progress to permanent residency or citizenship.
