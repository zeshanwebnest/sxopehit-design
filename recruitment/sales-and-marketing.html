Sales & Marketing

Grow Your Career in the Gulf's Retail & Digital Economy

Sales and marketing drive business growth by creating value and building lasting relationships — and today's digital economy means Gulf businesses need a strong online presence more than ever.

Roles for every strength. From sales executives and account managers to digital marketing specialists, brand managers and market researchers, we place candidates across field sales and office-based marketing functions.

A gateway to regional growth. The UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Bahrain's large retail and FMCG sectors offer real opportunities to build regional sales networks and grow into management roles.

  • Competitive, often tax-free compensation
  • Fast-growing retail & digital marketing sectors
  • Exposure to international brands & campaigns
  • Opportunities to build regional sales networks
  • Multicultural work environment across Gulf markets
  • Clear career growth into regional management

FAQs

Sales & Marketing Recruitment Questions

Openings span sales executives and account managers to digital marketing specialists, brand managers and market researchers, covering both field sales and office-based marketing functions.

Apply via our website's contact form, email or WhatsApp, and our recruitment team will follow up once your profile matches an employer requirement.

A business or marketing-related degree is typically expected, along with relevant sales or marketing experience; digital marketing certifications strengthen applications for online marketing roles.

Candidates can expect competitive, often tax-free pay, exposure to international brands, and faster career progression than is typical in many home-market roles.

Placement timing depends on employer demand for the specific role, but generally follows a sequence of CV screening, job matching, interview and documentation over a few weeks.
