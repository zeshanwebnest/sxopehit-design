Hospitality Industry

Join the Gulf's Booming Hospitality Sector

Middle East hospitality has expanded considerably over the past two decades, with Gulf countries now major hubs for business travel and tourism — driving strong demand for hotel, resort and F&B talent.

From front desk to management. We place candidates across housekeeping, front office, food & beverage, event planning, leisure and security departments.

A sector built for growth. Training and skill development are built into most roles, giving you a genuine path toward supervisory and management positions.

  • Strong, market-competitive salary packages
  • Tax-free or low-tax take-home income
  • Fast career growth in a booming tourism sector
  • Training & skill development opportunities
  • Exposure to multicultural guests & staff
  • Medical benefits & supportive work environment

Documents You'll Need

A typical hospitality placement requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your role at your free consultation.

  • Updated CV/resume
  • Valid passport
  • Educational certificates/hospitality diploma
  • Experience/reference letters
  • Food safety certification (F&B roles)
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Passport-size photographs
FAQs

Hospitality Recruitment Questions

Opportunities span housekeeping, front office, food & beverage, event planning, leisure/spa and security roles across hotels, resorts and guest houses throughout the region.

Submit your CV through our website, email or phone. Our recruiters screen and test candidates before matching them with hotel management for interviews.

Most roles require relevant experience and basic hospitality training; management and F&B roles benefit from formal diplomas or certifications. Good English communication is important.

Competitive, tax-free salaries, fast career advancement in a growing tourism market, training opportunities, and exposure to a multicultural work environment — advancement prospects are especially strong in Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, UAE and Bahrain.

After screening and testing, matching and interview coordination usually take a few weeks, with total placement time depending on visa processing and employer timelines.
