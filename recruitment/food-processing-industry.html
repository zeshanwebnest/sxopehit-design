Food Processing Industry

Join the Middle East's Growing Food Sector

The Middle East is a major hub for food production, manufacturing and processing, with heavy investment across Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Saudi Arabia creating stable, fast-growing careers.

Roles across the production line. We place candidates in production supervision, quality control, food safety inspection, food technology, maintenance engineering and supply chain roles.

A stable, growing sector. Food processing offers strong job security with real room for career advancement as the industry continues to expand.

  • Tax-free salary plus housing & medical coverage
  • Access to modern food processing technology
  • Strong job security in a fast-growing sector
  • Clear path for career advancement
  • Multicultural, supportive work environment
  • Exposure to international food safety standards

Documents You'll Need

A typical food processing placement requires the following — we confirm the exact list for your role at your free consultation.

  • Updated CV/resume
  • Valid passport
  • Food science/technology degree or diploma
  • Experience/reference letters
  • Food safety/HACCP certificate (if held)
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Passport-size photographs

Food Processing Recruitment Questions
