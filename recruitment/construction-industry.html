Construction Industry

Build Your Career on the Gulf's Biggest Projects

The Gulf's construction sector is undergoing rapid growth, creating strong demand for skilled trades and engineers on large-scale infrastructure projects across the region.

From site to supervisor. Whether you're an electrician, a civil engineer or a project manager, we place candidates across the full range of construction roles and seniority levels.

Work with modern equipment. Gulf construction sites use advanced machinery and tools, giving you hands-on experience that strengthens your CV for future roles.

  • Tax-free or low-tax salary
  • Pay often higher than equivalent roles in Pakistan
  • Work on large-scale, modern infrastructure projects
  • Access to advanced tools & equipment
  • Clear path to supervisory & management roles
  • Multicultural job sites & international experience

How We Work

Our Construction Placement Process

Our step-by-step procedure from your first CV submission to deployment.

01

Submit Your CV

Send your CV and trade certificates via our website, WhatsApp or email — we review your experience and credentials.

02

Screening

We assess your trade certification, PEC registration (for engineers) and performance record against live Gulf vacancies.

03

Matching & Interview

We match your profile to current openings and coordinate employer interviews.

04

Visa & Deployment

We handle visa processing, documentation and a pre-departure briefing before you deploy.

  • Valid passport
  • Educational certificates/diplomas
  • Trade test or skill certification

FAQs

Construction Recruitment Questions

Roles range from skilled trades — electricians, plumbers, carpenters — to engineers, foremen and project managers. Both entry-level and experienced professionals are placed depending on employer demand.

Submit your CV and credentials via our website, phone or email. Our recruiters screen your profile and match you to current Gulf vacancies before setting up employer interviews.

Requirements vary by role — tradesmen need relevant certification and experience, while engineers typically need a degree and PEC registration. Physical fitness for site work is required across most roles.

Workers earn tax-free or low-tax salaries, often higher than domestic pay, while working with modern equipment on large infrastructure projects and gaining international experience.

Timelines vary by role and employer demand, but typically range from a few weeks to a couple of months once documentation and visa processing are complete.
